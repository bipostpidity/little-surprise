07 · A LITTLE NOTE FOR YOU

What I really want to say…

A relationship can't survive on one person's effort. It needs two people.

If something about me is bothering you, tell me. If you're irritated with me, tell me. If I've hurt you, tell me. If you feel something has changed, tell me.

I’d rather hear a difficult truth from you than create a thousand explanations in my head.

I’m not asking for perfection. I just want us to remember that we're on the same side.

Thank you for being a part of my story. ❤️
